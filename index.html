Professional Services

Please note: Final pricing may vary depending on the amount of product used during your color service, as well as the length, density, and condition of your hair, and the overall duration of the service.

I will always provide a starting price estimate in advance when booking your appointment, so there are no surprises.

Custom Highlights

Tailored to your unique hair texture, density and color goals. Your custom highlight could include a blended lived in color and balayage with depth, a bright beach blonde or a more natural tone to show off your natural highlights.
This service includes highlighting, low lighting, gray blending, toning, wash, 5 minute head and neck massage, blow-dry, and style.

Haircut & Style

Full Service Haircut Includes Wash, 5 Minute Head and Neck Massage, Cut, Blow-dry, and Style.

Keratin Treatment 

Reducing frizz, adds shine and manageability. Restores the hair’s cuticle by sealing the protein into the hair for a smooth finish.

Tape-In Hair Extensions

To ensure you're extensions turn out absolutely perfect, I require a free consultation before booking that allows me to address your individual needs. All my hair is custom ordered. 

Blowouts

Wash, 5 Minute Head and Neck Massage, Blow-Dry, and Style.

"Fix My Hair" Color Correction

Had a color mishap or just not loving your current shade? My expert Color Correction service is designed to repair damage, remove unwanted tones, and restore your hair to a vibrant, healthy look you’ll love.
